New relative time formatting APIs:

- Adds `formatRelativeUtc`, `formatRelativeUnix`, `formatRelativeZoned`, `formatRelativeDate`, `formatRelativeDateTime`, and `formatRelativeTime` for human-friendly relative time output (e.g., "yesterday", "in 2 hours") across all supported types.

- Adds missing `formatUnix` and `formatUtc`.
